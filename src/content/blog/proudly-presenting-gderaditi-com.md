We are proud to announce the launch of our brand new in-house project - [gderaditi.com](http://gderaditi.com), an online platform that aims to simplify job seeking in the hospitality industry, streamlining the process for everyone involved. The initial version is focusing solely on Belgrade, Serbia, with new markets planned in the future.

So, how did [gderaditi.com](http://gderaditi.com) come to life? Are the current solutions not good enough?

Let's take a look at the hospitality industry in Serbia first.

The hospitality industry in Serbia has seen significant growth over recent years. In 2023, the market continued to expand due to increased tourism and investment in the sector. The total revenue generated by the hospitality industry in Serbia reached approximately EUR **1.5 billion** in 2023, marking a substantial increase compared to previous years. The sector contributed approximately **2.5%** to Serbia's GDP in 2023, underscoring its importance to the national economy. Beyond the numbers - people in Serbia like to eat good food and know how to enjoy their life. Cafés and restaurants are always full of people!

Still, even with all these positive news, a problem still remain - staff shortage, particularly for skilled positions.

That's where [gderaditi.com](http://gderaditi.com) comes in.

Hitting the sweet spot between being too low-tech and simple, and being too complex and restraining, the platform offers job seekers a very easy-to-use user experience, while not collecting any of their data, allowing them to fully browse the whole database without the need to register. Who needs **_ANOTHER_** password in their life, right?

![](/blog/proudly-presenting-gderaditi-com-_e64094d885cd47e0b13c22eeaf016f11mv2.jpg)

For the employers, the math is simple - [gderaditi.com](http://gderaditi.com) is an **affordable** and **modern** option of solving their staff shortage. The **affordable** part is especially true in the ongoing promo period - at the time of writing this blog post, the full ad package (platform ad and instagram promotion, lasting for two weeks) is 100% free for all employers in the hospitality industry!

We'll gladly keep working on the future iterations of the platform, and sincerely hope that you find it useful.
